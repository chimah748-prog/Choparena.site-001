Why escrow + two cross-checked screenshots works

⚖️

Dispute Path

If screenshots mismatch, payout is frozen and a structured dispute process begins with evidence review.

Dispute Path

What happens when screenshots mismatch

1. Payout automatically frozen

If the two screenshots do not match, ChopArena does not release escrow. Both players are notified immediately.

2. Evidence submission window

Both players may submit additional proof such as screen recording, full scoreboard, match code confirmation, or device timestamp.

3. Admin cross-check

ChopArena compares final score, player names, match code, and upload timing. The stronger consistent proof wins the review.

4. Resolution

If one side is clearly proven, escrow is released accordingly. If evidence is inconclusive, the match may be voided and stakes returned according to platform policy.
